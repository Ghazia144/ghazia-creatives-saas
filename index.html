FAQ

Questions, Answered

How does the process work?

Simply choose a design, fill in your wedding details, and preview your invitation live with your own information before making any payment. Once you complete the payment, Your final invite link will be sent to your whatsapp once payment is verified.

How long does payment verification take?

It takes Usually less than 1 hour.

Where can I find my invite link and order updates?

When you place an order, you get access to a private Order Status Page. This page will show if your payment is still being verified, and once it's approved, your live invitation link will be unlocked right there (along with being sent to your WhatsApp).

Can I make changes after my invite is delivered?

Absolutely. Every order includes 3 Free Edits. Once your invite is delivered, you will find a "Edit Invite" button on your Order Status Page. You can use it to make adjustments to your details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Direct Bank Transfer. You can pay from any Pakistani bank account, or directly from your JazzCash or Easypaisa app. After payment, simply share the payment proof screenshot and transaction ID with us. Your live invite link will be delivered to your WhatsApp immediately after the payment is verified.

Can I pay using JazzCash or Easypaisa?

Yes, absolutely. You can use the "Bank Transfer" feature inside your JazzCash or Easypaisa app to send the payment directly to our bank account.
