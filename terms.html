1. What we provide

Ghazia Creatives creates digital wedding invitations as private web pages. You choose a design, enter your wedding details and, after your payment is verified, you receive a private link that you can share with your guests, for example on WhatsApp. A live preview with your own details is available before you pay.

2. Orders and pricing

  • The price depends on the design and the number of events in your invitation. The exact amount is shown on the payment page before you pay.
  • Prices are in Pakistani Rupees (Rs).
  • An order is confirmed only after we have verified your payment.
  • Once payment is made, the design and plan cannot be changed. If you need a different design or more events, please place a new order or contact us on WhatsApp.

3. Payment and verification

  • We accept direct bank transfer. You can pay from any Pakistani bank account, or use the bank transfer feature inside your JazzCash or Easypaisa app.
  • Please pay only to the account details shown on our payment page. We are not responsible for money sent to any other account.
  • After paying, you must upload a clear screenshot of the payment and enter the transaction ID.
  • Verification usually takes less than 1 hour, and your invite link is delivered within 24 hours of a successful payment. Delays can happen during holidays or when our team is unavailable.
  • If a screenshot is unclear, a transaction ID is wrong or already used, or we cannot confirm the payment, we may reject the submission. We will tell you why on your order status page so that you can correct it.
  • Your invite link stays locked until your payment has been verified.

4. Delivery

Your invite link is shown on your private Order Status Page and sent to the WhatsApp number you entered in your order. Please check that the number is correct. We are not responsible if you entered a wrong number. In that case, contact us and we will help you with your link.

5. Edits

  • Every order includes 3 free edits after delivery. Each time you save a change to your delivered invitation counts as one edit. Saving without changing anything does not use an edit.
  • You can use your edits to update details such as names, dates, venues, times and your note.
  • If you need more than 3 edits, additional edits are paid. Message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you the rate.
  • Before payment, you can change your details as many times as you like.

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you enter, including spellings, dates, times, venue names and addresses, map links and phone numbers. Please review your live preview carefully before paying.

  • We may refuse an order, or remove an invitation, if it breaks these rules. In that case no refund will be due.
  • Please keep your order code and private link safe. Anyone who has your private order token can see and edit your order.

7. Refunds and cancellation

Because every invitation is custom-built from your details and delivered digitally, payments are not refundable once they have been approved and your invite has been delivered. However, we will refund you in full if:

  • we cannot deliver your invitation because of a problem on our side that we are unable to fix;
  • you were charged twice for the same order; or
  • you paid, but we could not accept or complete the order.

If you have not paid yet, you can leave the order at any time and nothing is charged. Unpaid orders may be deleted after some time. If you believe you are owed a refund, message us on WhatsApp with your order code. Approved refunds are returned to the account you paid from, and we will tell you the timeline when we confirm the refund.

8. How long your invitation stays online

Your invitation link will stay live for at least 6 months after the date of your last event. After that we may archive or remove it. If you would like to keep it for longer, please contact us before then. We aim to keep the service available all the time, but we depend on third-party hosting providers and cannot guarantee uninterrupted access.

What we collect. To make your invitation we collect the details you enter: the names of the couple and their fathers, event dates, times, venues and map links, your RSVP WhatsApp number, your note and your chosen music. We also collect your WhatsApp number, your email address if you provide it, your payment transaction ID and your payment screenshot. If you enter your email in the pop-up on our form page, we save it so that we can contact you about your order.

How we use it. We use your information only to create and deliver your invitation, verify your payment, provide support and keep order records. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

What guests can see. Your invitation is available to anyone who has your link. This includes the names, event details, venues and RSVP WhatsApp number that you put on the invitation. Please share the link only with people you want to see it. Your payment screenshot, transaction ID and your own contact details are never shown on the invitation.

Who handles your data. Your data is stored with trusted service providers that we use to run the website, such as our database and file storage, hosting and email services. Your payment screenshots are stored privately and can be viewed only by us.

Your browser. Our website saves your order code, private order token and form draft in your browser so that you can come back to your order. If you clear your browser data you may lose access to your order page. Contact us on WhatsApp and we will help you.

Keeping and deleting data. We keep your order data for as long as it is needed to provide the service, give support and keep our records. You can ask us to delete your data at any time by contacting us, and we will do so unless we need to keep something for a legitimate reason, such as an unresolved payment issue.
